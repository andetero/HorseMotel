HorseMotel Privacy Policy

The HorseMotel mobile app displays listing information provided by HorseMotel.com.

The app may request location access so it can show nearby listings, distances, and directions. Location is used on-device for app functionality.

Favorites are stored locally on the device.

HorseMotel.com remains the official website and source of truth for listings.

Listings are provided by HorseMotel.com and used with permission.
