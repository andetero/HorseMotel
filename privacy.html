HorseMotel.com App

Privacy Policy

HorseMotel.com is designed to help horse owners find overnight horse lodging while keeping the app simple, practical, and respectful of your privacy.

Information the app uses

The HorseMotel.com app displays lodging information provided by HorseMotel.com. The app may use your approximate device location to show nearby listings, sort by distance, and open directions in your preferred maps app.

Location access is optional and is used for app functionality. You can change location permissions at any time in your device settings.

Saved listings

Favorites and saved listings are stored locally on your device. They are not used to create an account profile by the app.

Contacting listings

When you choose to call, email, visit a website, or open directions, the app hands that action to the appropriate phone, mail, browser, or maps app on your device.

Listing issue reports

If you use the report issue option, your device opens an email addressed to HorseMotel.com. Any information sent is included only because you choose to send that email.

Data source and accuracy

HorseMotel.com remains the official website and source of truth for listings. Listings are provided by HorseMotel.com and used with permission.

Always contact a facility directly before arrival to confirm availability, pricing, directions, amenities, and current conditions.
